Create a brand new portfolio website for Jay Jay He from scratch using http://www.jayjayhe.com/ only as a reference for the existing logo, identity, and work themes. Do not copy the old layout or current template.

The goal is a top-tier Creative Director / Product Design leader portfolio: editorial, cinematic, minimal, confident, and highly custom. It should feel premium, authored, and senior — not like a generic template.

Position Jay Jay as a Creative Director focused on AI-driven data products, product design leadership, design systems, creative direction, and business impact.

Use this art direction:

Visual tone:
- Black / near-black foundation
- White and soft gray typography
- One restrained accent color only
- Minimal, precise, high-end
- Editorial, not startup-template
- Confident, not flashy

Typography:
- Use a modern grotesk or neo-grotesk font
- Hero headline: very large, tight, bold, 64–84px desktop
- H2: 36–48px
- Body: 16–18px
- Body line-height: 1.4–1.5
- Avoid loose paragraph spacing
- Typography should create authority and visual tension

Layout:
- Use a 12-column grid
- Max content width: 1280–1440px
- Desktop side padding: around 80px
- Mobile padding: around 24px
- Use asymmetric composition and varied spacing
- Avoid uniform stacked sections
- Use a rhythm of tight and loose spacing: 24 / 32 / 80 / 120 / 160px

Navigation:
- Sticky black top navigation that always stays visible while scrolling
- Height around 64–72px
- Larger, more prominent Jay Jay He logo on the left
- Nav items on the right: Work, About, Leadership, Contact
- Clean hover states and subtle active states
- Add a subtle bottom divider

Homepage hero:
- Make the hero bold, memorable, and leadership-driven
- Use a small overline, a large statement headline, and a short supporting sentence
- It should immediately signal Creative Director authority, product design leadership, AI/data platform experience, and business impact
- Avoid long intro paragraphs

Suggested placeholder hero direction:
“Designing AI-driven platforms that transform how data is understood and used.”
Supporting line:
“Creative Director and Product Design leader shaping systems, experiences, and strategy for complex digital products.”

Selected Work:
- Create a curated, editorial work section
- Use 1 large featured project and several smaller supporting projects
- Avoid a uniform card grid
- Use varied scale, strong image treatment, intentional cropping, and premium hover interactions
- Cards should include title, category, role, and short descriptor
- Use placeholder content and images for now, but make the structure easy to replace later

Case studies:
- Keep the current case study layout and structure if one already exists
- Preserve password-gated confidential case studies as a core feature
- Confidential projects must remain protected before users can access the full case study
- Case studies should support future content blocks for challenge, role, scope, process, impact, and visuals

Leadership / Approach:
- Add a strong section called “How I Lead Design” or “Leadership & Approach”
- Include 4 pillars:
  1. Product Thinking
  2. Systems & Scalability
  3. Team Leadership
  4. Business Impact
- Each pillar should be concise and visually refined
- This section must clearly signal seniority and Creative Director-level thinking

Motion / interaction:
- Use subtle, polished motion only
- Add smooth scroll behavior
- Add refined hover states
- Add tasteful fade/translate reveals
- Include one signature interaction, such as a staggered work-card reveal or elegant hero text reveal
- Avoid gimmicky animations or heavy parallax

Images:
- Do not use raw screenshot dumps as the design direction
- Frame project imagery in a cinematic, product-marketing style
- Use intentional cropping, scale, and dark editorial presentation

Technical:
- Fully responsive
- Accessible
- SEO-friendly
- Fast-loading
- Clean, maintainable code
- Easy to update with real project content, images, metrics, and writing later

The final result should feel like a personal brand website for a senior Creative Director who can lead product vision, design systems, teams, and business outcomes. It should be competitive with top-tier design leadership portfolios.
